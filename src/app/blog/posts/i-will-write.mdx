## Why this series exists

I want to turn what I learn into something visible, useful, and easier to remember. This series is my way of documenting the process of learning in public, one post at a time.

---

## What I’m aiming for

I’m planning to publish 43 posts in this series. Each post will capture a small part of what I’m studying, building, or figuring out so the writing stays practical instead of abstract.

---

## How often I’ll post

Whenever I can, with a goal of at least one post per week.

---

## What I hope this becomes

Over time, I want this to become a clear record of growth: the tools I use, the mistakes I make, and the lessons that stick. If the series works, it will help me think more clearly and write more confidently.
